Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Who we are

Nyovara is operated by NyotaCore Solutions & Services. Organisations using Nyovara act as data controllers for their crew records; NyotaCore processes that data on their behalf.

What we collect

Account details, crew and employment records entered by organisations or seafarers, documents uploaded to the platform, rest-hour records, welfare cases and technical logs needed to secure the service.

Sensitive information

Medical, welfare, payroll, identification and children’s information is restricted by role and scope, encrypted in transit and at rest, and access to confidential records is audited.

Platform staff access

NyotaCore staff cannot access tenant records without a documented reason, explicit scope, tenant approval and an expiry. Every action during support access is logged.

Retention

Records are retained according to the organisation’s configuration and applicable maritime and labour regulations. Historical records use soft deletion to preserve audit integrity.

Your rights

Seafarers may view their own records in the self-service portal and request corrections. Contact privacy@nyotacore.com for data-protection requests.
